How the system works: from sensor signal to alert

1. SenseVibration (accelerometer), acoustic (microphone) and light (4 photodiodes) are sampled together; vibration and sound at 12.8 kHz.
2. FilterBand-pass filters: vibration 10 Hz–2 kHz, acoustic 50 Hz–6 kHz.
3. Extract featuresEach 256 ms window becomes 128 numbers: band energies, mel energies, light statistics and cross-sensor correlations.
4. Classify on the deviceThe trained model outputs a probability for each of the five classes.
5. DecideAn alert is raised only if a threat class wins with confidence above 70%.
6. TransmitThe alert goes over LoRaWAN, or satellite if the gateway is unreachable, to this monitoring station.
